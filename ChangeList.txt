Version 2.5.0
- Tactical Display: improved drawing of units and labels (Settings / Tactical Display / Map drawing; master switch "Improved drawing of units and labels", on by default; off: the game's own drawing, also for the compact sonobuoys and the cut contact ellipses):
  - "Label position" (Bottom by default; Game: the game's own placement): the text of a unit is put on the chosen side of its icon, centred on it, just outside the drawn icon (and the selection frame). The lines are aligned by the side: centred above and below, right on the left side, left on the right side.
  - "Filter overlapping labels" (on): the labels are placed in order (the selected unit, the formation leaders and names, the other units, biggest first); a label that would overlap one already placed or an icon tries the opposite side, then the other two, and only then loses lines from the bottom; one that fits nowhere is not drawn. The selected unit's label is never cut. A label keeps its place for a second while it still fits, so the labels of moving units do not jump back and forth. An own unit with room for one line only shows its second line (the name).
  - "Formation name only" (on): an unselected formation shows its name instead of the labels of its units; the labels of its ships are added last, where there is room. In a selected formation of aircraft only the selected aircraft has a label.
  - The name of a formation is placed around the whole formation, not at its leader. A unit farther from its leader than the "Formation spread limit" (ships and ground units 5 nm, aircraft 1 nm) is an independent unit with its own label.
  - "Aircraft formation: distance and altitude" (on): under the name of an unselected flight, the distance and altitude of its leader.
  - "Formation lines" (on): with a unit of a formation selected, lines from the leader to every unit of the formation.
  - "FRND badge in the label" (on): the FRND badge of a friendly unit of another side is a line of its label, placed and cut with it, instead of a badge beside the icon that covered other labels.
  - Empty lines inside unit labels are removed and the label boxes follow the text they show (no gaps above or between the lines).
- Tactical Display: "Fine zoom with Shift" (Settings / Tactical Display / Map, on by default): with Shift held the mouse wheel zooms in steps 5 times smaller.
- Main menu (Settings / Game Menus / Save and load, on by default): "Open Load game on entering Scenarios and Campaign"; "Menu windows beside the buttons": the Load game window and the other windows of the menu are put beside the menu buttons, not over them; "File picker without a height limit".
- Unit Status: a unit without a tab of its own opens on the tab you chose last; the tab the game falls back to on a unit that lacks it no longer replaces your choice.
- Desktop mode, other monitors: the Formation Manager's right-click menu works there; the close button and the drawing toolbar of a Tactical Display there follow the game as on the main screen.
- Loading screen: the line with the loaded mods is in a dark panel like the game's tip, sharp on any picture.

- Speed: property reads of the mod are delegates instead of reflection; the Unit Status helpers run 20 times a second instead of every frame; the sonobuoy count walks the interface tree every 2 s (not 2 times a second); closed windows are skipped by more steps; "is it a map window" is remembered.
- Memory: the backdrop picture of the World View (texture and sprite) is freed when the backdrop goes; the loadout tables of the Unit Reference are no longer kept for good; the picture chosen in Create Mod is freed when another is chosen; the list of tracked windows is cleaned.
- Desktop mode: while a mission is loading the World View picture was set up and taken down again on every frame (photo mode flickering in the trace); it now waits for the mission.
- Windows with an opacity / scale / text contrast look: the whole window was walked once a second (a hitch each second, also on a pause, and also for closed windows); now it is walked only when the layout changed, and at least every ten seconds. A closed window no longer keeps the screen capture of "text by background" going.
- Outside a mission: the cursor callback is put again on a change of screen and every ten seconds (was every second); the "text by background" capture is taken 5 (small) / 2 (full) times a second instead of 10 / 4. The trace is written by a thread of its own (a file write on the game's thread could stall a frame).
- The look of a window is no longer redone on every LayoutUpdated (it fires all the time in the menus - a hitch each second): the size and children count of the window's panel are compared once a second instead.
- Outside a mission the "text by background" capture is taken four times less often (0.8 s small / 2 s full): the full-screen capture is a GPU cost the CPU profile does not show.
- Video options: the game's Frame Rate Limit slider goes up to 250 instead of 120; the limit is set there, in the game's own option. The mod keeps it precisely (a timed wait instead of the engine's coarse one; vertical sync off).
- Video options: a check box "Remove frame rate limit" under that slider (also Settings / Windows / Performance / "Remove frame rate limit"): no limit at all, the slider is switched off; the game's value stays and comes back when it is off.
- Video options: "Adaptive V-Sync" in the V-Sync list (Settings / Windows / Performance / "Add Adaptive V-Sync", on): vertical sync is on while the frames keep up with the screen's refresh rate and off while they do not (back on when they are well inside it). It is saved as ordinary V-Sync, so the game works without the mod. Not used when the game's frame limit is below the refresh rate.
- Menu slideshow: the next picture is read and decoded five seconds beforehand by a web request (a worker thread) and put into the game's texture caches; the game's own loader decoded it on the main thread (a 70-80 ms frame at each change). If it is not ready in time the game loads it as before.
- In a mission (264 windows on the screen): the open / closed state of the windows is taken from the window's IsVisibleChanged event instead of being asked every frame, and the Damage Control fit skips windows that are not shown: about 0.6 ms per frame less.
- A window that is not on the screen is no longer walked through by every step each frame: it is looked at when it tells (IsVisibleChanged / Loaded / Unloaded) only.
- Debug = true: every 10 seconds the trace gets a "profile:" line - the average frame time and the milliseconds per frame the mod's steps and patches take (to find what costs FPS).
