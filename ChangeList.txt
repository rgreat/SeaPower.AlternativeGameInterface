Version 1.7.0
- New option "Map and World View only in windows": Tactical Display and World View become ordinary free windows (move, size, snap, any order and level). The 3D picture is rendered by the game's main camera into a texture of the World View window's own size, pixel for pixel (the game's PiP supersampling option is honoured), so it lies in the window like any other content. Each window is opened and closed on its own (the X of World View, the "World View" item in the Tactical Display's menu, the HUD button / key of the map). Behind the windows: a topographic backdrop picture (SmartWindows_Backdrop.jpg next to the dll, replace it with any picture). Backspace (photo mode) shows the 3D view on the whole screen. The compass tape moves into the World View window and is white or black by the brightness of the picture behind it.
- Window look menu (the arrow next to the gear): opacity, scale and level (bottom / default / top) of a window.
- Docked windows can be resized from every edge: at the screen edge the resize zone reaches a few pixels inside the window.
- The time panel of the mission can be dragged (double-click puts it back).
- Unit Status: "Track State" tab for a selected contact of another side.
- Campaign: "Upgrade ship..." button in the Task Force Builder; damaged ships can be dismissed (the repair cost is deducted); loadouts are sorted by date; the price of a hull swap is counted from the roster.
- The News window of the main menu gets its gear, frame and remembered position; the game's resize cursors work outside a mission.
- Supports Sea Power 0.8.6 - 0.8.7.
- (1.6.2) "Backgrounds from mods only" works with Sea Power 0.8.7; Strict loadouts no longer offers ships whose loadout the campaign has not opened yet.
