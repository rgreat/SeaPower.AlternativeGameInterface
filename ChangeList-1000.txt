Version 2.6.0
- One settings window for all four Alternative mods:
  - Find any option by typing its name in the search box.
  - Switch between the mods' settings with one click; your edits are kept.
  - Choose the language once for all mods.
  - Undo changes you have not applied yet.
- Long settings tabs (Windows, Game Menus) are split into pages, so options are easier to find.
- The settings window opens with only the settings of the window you came from; "Show all settings" is remembered until you quit the game.
- Each desktop remembers the World View camera offset.
- Text-by-background switching thresholds are now options.
