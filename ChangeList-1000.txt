Version 1.7.0
- New desktop mode: Tactical Display and World View are ordinary free windows. The 3D view is rendered pixel for pixel into the World View window; backdrop picture behind the windows (SmartWindows_Backdrop.jpg, replaceable); The minimap key (Tab) switches 2-5 desktops (window positions, visibility, look), the active one is remembered; "Desktops" settings page; World View on/off key; Backspace: 3D view fullscreen; compass tape inside the window, white or black by brightness.
- Window look menu: opacity, scale, level (map, 3D: Bottom).
- Docked windows can be resized from every edge.
- Draggable time panel, kept per desktop.
- Keep map view on resize (option).
- Unit Status: Track State tab.
- Event Log: all tracks are links; a click centres the map.
- Campaign: Upgrade ship, dismiss damaged, loadouts by date.
- News window gets gear, frame, remembered position.
- Backgrounds from mods and Strict loadouts fixes.
- AddWindowKey (F12) removed.
- Sea Power 0.8.6 - 0.8.7.
